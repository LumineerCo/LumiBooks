Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 · LumiBooks by LumineerCo

1. Data We Collect

We collect name, email, phone, and business information provided during registration. Financial data is stored in a private Google Sheet created exclusively for your account.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Each user gets a dedicated Google Sheet on the LumiBooks service account. It is never shared with any other user. The master database stores only email, hashed password, plan status, and a reference to your sheet.

3. Passwords

Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 with a server-side salt. We never store or can retrieve plain-text passwords.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal or business data to any third party.

5. Sessions

We use secure tokens stored in your browser's localStorage. No cookies are used for tracking.

6. Third-Party Services

LumiBooks uses Google Sheets API (Google Cloud) as its database backend.

7. Data Deletion

Contact lumineerco@yahoo.com to request account deletion within 7 days.
